# BCENV-012: replacement preflight and live launch

This is a public engineering and launch checkpoint, not a candidate outcome or
competence claim. The [prospective protocol](2023-continuity-capacity-feasibility-012.md)
remains byte-identical to its prelaunch version. The competitive goal is not achieved.

## Two distinct preflights

**012-A remains failed and preserved.** Its eight official-tutorial games finished,
but feedback retrieval exposed the consumer's incorrect treatment of legitimate
atomic-publication staging files. The uncertain request was not resent. The repair
preserves canonical bounded staging files until the unchanged client commits them;
it does not change the engine, client, schedules or resolved capacity limits.
A real-client regression fails the old consumer and passes the repaired code.

**012-B passed as a separately authorized eight-game preflight.** These are eight
new engineering games, not a repair or relabeling of A: A and B together consumed
16 games. B used one unchanged official-tutorial source submission, no validation
phase, no replacement submission and no automatic retry. Existing 009 phase-state
proof was referenced rather than rerun. Independent retained-evidence review found:

- Eight completed games; all eight replay hashes match the corresponding 009
  tutorial evidence. This is repeatability evidence, not a competitive result.
- One note and one version; 23 result polls, 39 replay requests, 64 total read
  debits and 66 mailbox/dispatch attempts. Staging files incurred no false debit.
- Legacy-action agreement and 89 own-diagnostic rows agreeing with retained raw
  vectors. Both slots, sampled action/movement activity and terminal/source-round
  coverage were checked. Three early action windows were **not exhausted** by the
  two-page limit; this is not complete all-round or all-field replay enumeration.
- All 55 known child receipts (16 engine/initial-summary and 39 query children)
  report success and reaping before the preflight deadline. This is not a global
  process-absence claim.
- 374 successful storage checkpoints, with maximum sampled high-water usage of
  5,588,064 bytes. The 1 GiB guard is sampled detection/admission, not a hard disk
  quota; conservative scan-race failures and in-flight overshoot remain possible.

The coordinator's redundant observer lookup failed on an absent top-level field;
it is not counted as a successful check. The actual trusted launcher/probe gates
and independently checked identities passed. The driver terminated successfully;
the owned service PTY terminated with exit 1 after operator interruption, without
a graceful-stop marker. The exit-code mapping cause is not established. These
limits remain part of the evidence rather than being described as graceful shutdown.
The final 972-file frozen inventory matches the independently observed snapshot.
