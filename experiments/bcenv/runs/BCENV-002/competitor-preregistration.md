# Experiment 001: launcher range discipline

Registered after all four baseline games and first150-round bounded queries, before variant code. Baseline analysis: work/baseline-analysis.json and work/analyze.py. Baseline wins4/4; length372,324,171,190 rounds for DefaultA/DefaultB/SmallA/SmallB. This already limits any claim of a win-rate improvement.

Observation: in fixed rounds1–150, candidate launcher recorded positions had another opposing launcher at squared distance<=20 on114 robot-round observations;50 of these were at distance<=8 (43.86%). By paired condition the close/engagement counts were12/18,0/0,24/62,14/34. DefaultB supplies no eligible first150 engagement observations. All selected queries are complete, untruncated, source complete, and cover every requested positive round. These are geometric co-presence measurements, not robot visibility. Raw examples: baseline-default-A round137 event138 candidate13123 row11 vs opponent13646 row21 distance8; candidate13099 row20 vs opponent10765 row23 distance5. baseline-small-A round102 event103 candidate10057 row8 vs opponent10155 row7 distance4. See query directories for exact records. These observations and source review motivate testing over-closing, but do not prove intent, bad tactics, or the cause of deaths.

Falsifiable hypothesis: changing only launcher movement against an already in-range visible enemy launcher from greedy pursuit to a desired squared standoff distance13 will lower the pooled fraction of close geometric launcher encounters by at least25% relative (from50/114=0.438596 to<=0.328947) in rounds1–150, with reductions in at least two of the three baseline-eligible paired conditions. Maintain at least half of baseline aggregate eligible engagement observations (>=57) to avoid declaring success by eliminating encounters. Candidate total LAUNCH_ATTACK events in rounds1–150 across all four conditions must remain>=80% of baseline339 (=at least272), and win count must remain4/4. All conditions must pass to keep; otherwise reject variant and select baseline. Death counts will be reported descriptively but are not a claim of attack attribution or a preregistered survival endpoint.

Focused intervention: when selected closest-priority enemy is a launcher and within squared16, choose staying or a legal step minimizing abs(distanceSquared-13), with a strong penalty for enemy HQ danger. No changes to production, mining, anchoring, target choice, other navigation, or exploration. Repeat the exact four map/side conditions, same pinned opponent and engine, one game per condition; no seed search. The first150 window is common to all baseline games and will not be moved after seeing retests. If a variant game ends before150, requested-round coverage cannot establish the planned endpoint and the primary test is inconclusive/rejected rather than extrapolated.

Analysis script work/analyze.py was written before variant; it checks completeness and computes recorded-position distances without inferring within-round action ordering. End-round movement vectors can reflect currents; they do not directly measure the chosen movement or bot knowledge. Exposure denominators depend on trajectory and population and aren't independent trials. This experiment tests one implementation on one educational opponent and two maps, not general combat strength.
