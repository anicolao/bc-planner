# Initial behavior plan (recorded before candidate code)

Fresh independent candidate; no inherited source. Plan: maintain a modest mining economy, acquire islands with standard anchors, and use launchers to contest the center. HQs register locations in shared memory, build up to eight carriers each, spend mana on launchers, and after round 120 reserve resources for one stockpiled anchor. Carriers choose a home HQ, preferentially mine mana (two thirds) or adamantium (one third), return full loads, and empty carriers collect anchors and seek neutral islands. Sensed wells/islands are shared only when write permission allows. Launchers attack in-range low-health military targets before carriers, greedily approach visible enemies, otherwise move through center and reflected home-HQ guesses. Movement uses legal neighbor checks and short recent-position penalties; this is not complete pathfinding. No map files are inspected for strategic information.
