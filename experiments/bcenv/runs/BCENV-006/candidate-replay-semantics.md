# Bounded replay evidence

Replay queries select inclusive round bounds and optionally a robot ID, with a record offset and limit. Use the returned `feedback.coverage.next_record_offset` for continuation, keeping the same round window and robot filter. Do not use `source_next_record_offset` for continuation: output may have been reduced to fit the response bound. A null continuation only closes the selected record window; it does not prove every requested round exists. Inspect returned coverage and completeness fields, including source-stream and requested-round coverage.

Records are extracted official replay arrays, not reconstructed world state or a robot's visibility/knowledge. Presentation order groups event types and does not establish chronology across groups within a round. Movement positions do not establish movement intent or cause. Action codes and raw targets do not establish damage, hits, or collection. Team resource rows are net team deltas, not gross collection, spending, balances or robot-local resources; both teams' rows remain under robot filtering. Missing or unvalidated fields are not evidence of absence. Initial bodies use synthetic round zero. Do not infer death causes from death rows.

Returned counts can span the entire validated selected stream even when the displayed page is bounded. Page completeness, selected-window completeness, source-stream completeness and coverage of requested rounds are distinct claims. Retain query parameters, source/replay hashes and returned replay-reference IDs alongside conclusions. Link IDs in later prospective notes if relevant. These notes explain interface evidence limits, not game strategy.
